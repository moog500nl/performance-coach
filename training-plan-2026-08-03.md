## ☀️ The heat plan (Mon–Tue)

With only 2 qualifying outdoor rides in the last 14 days (22.6 °C and 25.0 °C), your thermal baseline is thin, so absolute tiers apply: a 30 °C+ day is **Tier 2 — active session modification**. Your indoor setup (one fan, no AC) is *not* a refuge — an uncooled 28 °C room loads you harder than 30 °C outdoors at speed.

How the week absorbs it:

- **Monday takes the worst heat as a rest day.** Zero training cost.
- **Tuesday is Z2 only — the one session type heat barely touches** if you respect one rule: **HR is the ceiling (131), power floats down.** Ride the coolest window you have — outdoors before ~10:00 or after ~19:30 (HR 120–131, cap 135), or indoors with the fan on max using the heat-managed file. Power sagging 10–15 W below normal Z2 at capped HR is the protocol working, not a lost session.
- **No quality in the heat.** The week's hard session waits until Wednesday. If the heatwave lingers into Wednesday, the interval rule is **cut volume, keep power**: 3×12 at a true 179 W, never 4×12 at a sagging target.
- **Fluids:** ~750 ml/h with sodium on both hot-day rides, starting in the first 15 minutes. Evening rehydration on Tuesday decides Wednesday's interval quality.
- Small silver lining: controlled Z2 heat exposure ≥60 min *is* heat acclimatization — useful for warm MDS stages — and adaptation starts landing within 3–5 days.
