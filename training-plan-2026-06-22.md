## How this week works

The whole week is built around the heat. Afternoon highs run 24–33 °C, but those are peaks — at 06:00–08:00 even the hottest days start around 16–19 °C, so riding outdoors early sidesteps the problem entirely. Two principles set the shape:

1. **The big day goes on the coolest morning.** Thursday is the only sub-25 °C day, so the 3.5 h long ride lands there. The one quality session (Zwift sweet spot) sits Monday on rested legs.
2. **The 33 °C days stay light.** Tuesday is an easy spin, Saturday a short dawn shake-out, and Sunday is the firm rest day — so the heat never collides with a hard effort.

Only Monday is indoors, and only because sweet spot is far easier to hold by power than by heart rate on Amsterdam's flat roads. Everything else is outside on the Condor, paced by HR.

## Heat protocol

- **Roll out by ~07:00**, dawn (~06:30) on the 33 °C days, and aim to be home before ~09:30 when it climbs fast.
- **Hydration:** 500–750 ml/h. Pre-drink ~500 ml before heading out, and add electrolytes/sodium to a bottle on Thursday's long ride and on the two hot days.
- **Thursday (3.5 h):** carry 2–3 bottles (plan a refill stop), plus sunscreen, light kit and food. If you run dry early or your HR won't settle under the cap, cut it to 3 h — it's still the week's anchor.
- **Pace by HR, not speed** — on warm mornings you'll be slower at the same heart rate, and that's correct.
- **Stop and cool down** if you get a headache, chills, stop sweating, or your HR stays pinned high at easy effort.
