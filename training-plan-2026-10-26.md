## Session notes

- **Tuesday** is the day after leg press. Expect HR in the high 110s at 130 W. If HR sits above 130 for 10 minutes or the quads are properly sore, set the Zwift workout intensity to 92% and finish there.
- **Wednesday** is technique, not power. ERG holds 130 W; your job is the rpm. Smooth circles, quiet upper body. If you bounce on the saddle, drop 5 rpm and build back. It is preparation for the ramp test, which rewards being at home at 95 to 100 rpm once the steps get heavy.
- **Saturday** adds 30 minutes to last week and lifts the last block to 140 W, the top of the Z2 band we use until the test. Fuel to the clock: something every 20 minutes, about 150 g of carbs in total, 500 to 750 ml an hour, fan on from the start. If HR passes 135 in the last block, set intensity to 95%.
