- **Monday gate:** last night was 5h25m. If tonight is short again *and* HRV drops below ~29 (10% under baseline), trim Monday's ride to 60 min and keep it at the bottom of Z2 (≤125 bpm) — it's an aerobic-volume day, not a test.
- **Wednesday quality gate (the big one — it now sits the morning after the first loaded walk):** green or single-amber *and* legs/feet feel normal → 3×15 as written. Two ambers, or noticeable foot/hip/calf soreness from Tuesday's pack walk → 3×12 (the file marks the bail-out at 12 min per interval). HRV red / RHR +5 → skip quality, ride Thursday's steady file instead and drop Thursday. Per the dossier, loaded-walk fatigue shows in the morning numbers before it shows as pain — trust them.
- **Saturday is the load dial.** If the week has felt heavy or Friday readiness is borderline, ride 3 h instead of 3.5 (≈ −18 TSS, ACWR back under ~1.25). Rain → the 175-min indoor fallback file. Don't extend past 3.5 h — the deload is next week anyway.
- **Foot check after Tuesday's walk:** any hot spot, rub or blister → flag it now. Blisters are the #1 MDS DNF cause and the whole point of starting loaded walks 14 weeks out is finding these problems while they're cheap.
- **Don't add to Friday or Sunday.** The rest days are what make a 306-TSS week with a new walking stimulus safe.
