## Return rules (Fri 6 to Sun 8)

- **Morning check before each ride:** a temperature, a tight chest, or RHR 58 or higher means rest that day.
- **In-ride check:** your HR runs about 10 bpm above normal for the watts (Friday: above 118 at 115 W; Saturday: above 125 at 125 W), or the effort sets off a cough. Either one: stop at 20 minutes and rest the next day.
- A leftover dry cough at rest is common and not a reason to stop on its own. A cough that gets worse with effort is.
- If Friday or Saturday fails the check, rest Sunday as planned and ride Friday's file again on Monday before doing Gym A.
- If you had a fever, or the cough or breathlessness is getting worse rather than better, check with your GP before training.

## Readiness and ACWR next week

The daily readiness check may say "modify" next week, and ACWR will read close to 2 by Saturday. That is arithmetic: eight days off collapsed the 28-day base, so an ordinary 6 to 7 h Z2 week looks like a spike. Go by HRV, RHR and how the rides feel instead. If RHR is 58 or higher two mornings running, or HRV stays at 21 or below, drop Friday's ride first, then cut Saturday to 90 minutes.
